## MODEL CHANGES

head: sampled anatomical skull fields and corrected the erroneous 20 mm head
detail attachment offset. Hair now follows a varying forehead/temple/nape line.

face: orbital bowls, brow arches, cheek planes, maxilla, philtrum and chin
fields; fitted sclera/iris surfaces, paired upper/lower lids, deviated nose
bridge/tip and alar wings, nostril slits, lips and mouth corners. Uneven brows,
a healed brow scar, restrained jaw/cheek differences and varied ears establish
an authored identity. Ear helix silhouette, concha and antihelix are separate
forms. No face-expression rig was added.

neck: paired SCM implication, suprasternal notch and trapezius fields.

torso: paired pectoral masses, sternal channel, lower pec transition, broad
abdominal wall, obliques, restrained serratus and back/scapular fields.

shoulders: clavicular torso weights now include shoulder-girdle ownership;
inner deltoid weights blend toward chest. Deltoid/arm forms and corrective
volume offsets replace reliance on support loops alone.

arms: directional forearm mass, inner-arm flattening, wrist taper and retained
olecranon detail, sampled through continuous anatomical fields.

hands/gloves: shared opponent/player mitt construction; directed compression
folds, contact-surface wear, higher-resolution thumb/cuff silhouettes and a
pose-driven compression morph. Existing thumb web, cuff overlap, wraps, lacing
and welt remain the construction foundation.

trunks: distinct front/back panels, localized tension folds, gathered waistband,
shaped thick hem, side piping and inseam. Upper panel vertices blend pelvis and
thigh ownership while hems stay thigh-local. Bind and flexion tests cover both
legs, including the right attachment's rotation.

legs: quadriceps, inner knee/adductor, posterior calf split and hamstring fields;
bounded hip/knee corrective offsets supplement existing landmarks.

boots: ankle flex creases, restrained side variation, smoother instep shaping
and localized toe wear. Existing tongue, lacing, sole rocker, heel and foot
contact system were retained.

skin: zero metalness, subtle regional warmth and sweat/dryness response for
opponent, face and first-person skin. Standard-material hooks compose with
Phase 1; no new procedural texture is allocated by this pass. True soft tissue
light transport was not added or claimed.

corrective deformation: ten relative skin position/normal targets, prebuilt
once, with offsets bounded to 4-12 mm per target and influence bounded to 0-1.
Gloves have a separate bounded compression target. Runtime corrective updates
change preallocated scalars; this is not a claim that all legacy animation
code allocates nothing. Multiple active corrections may overlap.

stance: existing asymmetric guard, planted foot ownership and pelvic load are
preserved. Inspection adds neutral, wide/close base and deep squat stress poses.

motion: existing jab/cross extension/recovery timing, kinetic chain and connected
head/body reactions are preserved. Skin, garment and glove deformation now
respond to those poses. No combat timing, AI or damage rule was changed.

first-person model: reshaped flexor/extensor and wrist forms, shared glove
fidelity, regional material response and pose-driven forearm/glove compression.
Existing idle, block and punch framing requires rendered validation.

## MODEL INSPECTION SYSTEM

Open http://127.0.0.1:5180/?validation=models. The query explicitly enables the
inspection mode in development or production builds. Without it, production
presentation is unchanged. The dev server command is
`npm run dev -- --host 127.0.0.1 --port 5180 --strictPort`.

states available: 17 opponent states and 8 first-person states, listed in the
separate Gemini prompt and `validation.models.states`.

```js
const m = window.__SUMO_IS_A_BOXER__.validation.models;
m.show('front_neutral');
m.focus('face');
m.orbit(45);
m.show('cross_extension');
m.play();
m.pause();
m.scrub(0.5);
m.lighting('ring'); // or 'neutral'
m.controls(false);
m.metrics();
await m.sample(5000);
window.__SUMO_IS_A_BOXER__.validation.diagnostics();
```

All states use deterministic 120 Hz presentation rehearsal and a fixed match
seed without stepping combat. Normal game controls/audio are inactive; HUD,
damage overlay, particles, flashes and environment geometry are suppressed.
Full orbit, face/torso/body focus, motion scrubbing and neutral/ring light
comparison expose the character. Playback loops include deterministic settling
work, which is included in inspector CPU timing and should not be mistaken for
ordinary combat update cost. First-person close-ups deliberately use a narrower
inspection FOV; the other first-person states use gameplay FOV.

## KNOWN LIMITATIONS

- No rendered verification. The visual quality, shader GPU compilation, browser
  stability and GPU budget remain pending. Do not certify premium readiness.
- Skin is composed of intersecting loft surfaces, not a single welded topology.
  Shoulder/armpit/neck continuity still needs harsh inspection under motion.
- Scalar joint-angle corrective activation is approximate, not a full
  directional pose-space system or dual-quaternion skinning solution.
- Garments have deterministic skeletal deformation, not cloth dynamics or
  collision. Deep flexion may still expose panel intersections.
- Facial anatomy is static. There is no expression, blink, jaw or eye-tracking rig.
- Regional standard-material response is not subsurface scattering.
- First-person clipping/FOV, boots on canvas and material response under actual
  warehouse lighting require independent rendered review.
- The local server is available for review. Git push is not a public deployment.
