# Voxel art direction

**Status:** IMPLEMENTED as project law for VOXEL-PIVOT-001.

## Laws

1. **SUMO SHAPE.** Huge torso and abdomen, thick waist, broad pelvis, heavy glutes, enormous thighs, thick neck, low centre of gravity. No bodybuilder V-taper. No conventional boxer physique.
2. **HIGH DENSITY.** The hero is a high-resolution voxel sculpture, not a handful of blocks.
3. **SMOOTH MACRO FORM.** At gameplay distance the silhouette reads as a coherent 3D figure.
4. **VISIBLE CUBIC MICROSTRUCTURE.** At close range the cubes are unmistakable.
5. **NO MINECRAFT.** Not coarse cubic worlds, not 16³ characters.
6. **NO ROBLOX.** Not rounded plastic primitives.
7. **NO BOXING MOTIF.** No gloves, trunks, boots, ropes, turnbuckles, canvas, corner padding, or red/blue corner dressing as visual identity.
8. **NO SMOOTH CHARACTER AS FINAL RENDER.** The certified guide mesh is hidden. It may be shown in `?validation=voxel` GUIDE / WIREFRAME modes only.
9. **CLAY / SILHOUETTE TRUTH.** Macro mass and silhouette outrank surface polish.
10. **REFERENCE IMAGES ARE AUTHORITATIVE.** Approved files in `references/visual/` override provisional numbers in code.

This is **not** a pixel shader on a smooth mesh, and **not** cubes glued onto skin. Occupancy is sampled from the guide surface.
